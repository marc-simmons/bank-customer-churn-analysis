\# Bank Customer Churn Analysis



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes customer churn behavior for a retail banking institution using Microsoft Excel.



The objective was to identify factors associated with customer attrition and provide recommendations to improve customer retention.



\## Tools Used



\- Microsoft Excel

\- Data Cleaning

\- Pivot Tables

\- Business Analysis

\- Data Visualization



\## Analysis Performed



\### Data Cleaning

\- Reviewed customer records for missing and invalid values

\- Verified balance, tenure, and product fields

\- Standardized data for analysis



\### Customer Activity Analysis

Analyzed the relationship between customer activity status and churn behavior.



\### Credit Score Analysis

Compared average credit scores between retained and exited customers.



\### Product Ownership Analysis

Evaluated how the number of products held by customers influenced churn rates.



\### Decision Tree Visualization

Created a decision tree to illustrate the relationship between customer activity, credit score, product ownership, and churn risk.



\## Key Findings



\- Inactive customers demonstrated substantially higher churn rates than active customers.

\- Customers with lower credit scores were more likely to leave the bank.

\- Customers holding fewer products exhibited higher churn rates.

\- Customer engagement appeared to be the strongest predictor of retention.



\## Business Recommendations



\- Increase engagement campaigns targeting inactive customers.

\- Develop retention programs for customers with declining activity.

\- Encourage cross-selling of additional banking products.

\- Implement early-warning monitoring for customers exhibiting churn indicators.
